# LC native framebuffer to independent 480i canvas

Ported in the existing `master` checkout based on LC HEAD
`045f8967aceaf5fc91f152f20b0b9e60e30efddf`. No commit, worktree, Quartus
invocation, build-stamp change, deployment or hardware operation was made.

The verified source is the clean Quadra `157CRT` checkout at
`0de453bc86e9d67061ee2b43ca8c08b8c9d39689` (`480i-quadra-test-failed-timing`),
whose predecessor is the supplied `a42ea84` baseline. The work described as
uncommitted in the original handoff had been committed by the time of this
port. Quadra source files were read only. Its latest STA reports have major
setup/hold failures, including TV setup -4.971 ns and hold -0.533 ns;
they do not establish timing success for this design or for LC.

## Selected profile and preserved raster

`MacLC.qsf` selects `MAC_TV525_DIAG=1`, with `BUFFERED=1` in `sys_top`.
HDMI/ASCAL stays enabled; `MISTER_DEBUG_NOHDMI` remains commented out.
The CRT-only variant is also route-checked. No audio feature was disabled.

All thirteen portable RTL files are byte-identical to the verified Quadra
source: capture, FIFO, frame store, buffered canvas, burst arbiter, timing,
pattern, OSD, component arithmetic/pins, output, platform and TV PLL.
Both QSF and QIP lists include the complete dependency set. Their source
hashes are in `scratch/tv_port_source.sha256`.

The Sony-qualified diagnostic raster remains the invariant: independent
27 MHz TV clock, first temporal field drawing odd canvas rows, one complete
image selected at `frame_start` for both fields, independent TV OSD, full
eight-bit component DAC lanes and authoritative composite sync on HS.
The analog clock DDIO uses the dedicated TV clock directly. Guest reset
does not restart the raster. Startup uses the diagnostic fallback; source
loss repeats the last valid image; late DDR data blacks a complete line
without stalling sync. Buffered RGB and tags retain their six-stage latency.

## Actual LC tap

LC uses `maclc_v8_video`, rather than DAFB. The actual monitor cases are:

| Monitor ID | Active size | Native totals | TV treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 512x384 | 640x407 | Centered at canvas (64,48) |
| 6/default | 640x480 | 800x525 | Canvas (0,0) |
| 1 | 640x870 | 832x918 | Invalid capture geometry (0x0) |

The misleading ID-1 source comment calls that case a 12-inch monitor;
its implemented counters actually produce the portrait raster above.
Neither those counters nor monitor selection was changed.

V8 exports first-active-pixel line/frame markers and geometry through the
same two ungated register stages as palette RGB and DE. Marker generation
is suppressed during native reset. Geometry derives from the internally
synchronized `monid_v`, including explicit rejection of the portrait case
instead of truncating 870 into the nine-bit capture height.

`MacLC.sv` exports `{v8_vga_r,v8_vga_g,v8_vga_b}`, `v8_de`, markers,
geometry and `vidrst_s`. The shared CE is `v8_ce_pix`. This tap precedes
MT32 LCD/HUD, `video_freak`, scanlines and normal OSD. Native PLL control,
VRAM reads, palette output, interrupts, guest monitor configuration and
audio routes remain unchanged. TV OSD is composed downstream of canvas
scanout. The desktop simulator difference is recorded in
`docs/verilator_differences.md`.

## LC memory and framework integration

LC's ASCAL base is 0x20000000, with three 8 MiB slots ending at 0x21800000
(small-vbuf uses less). Its core DDRAM port belongs to Ethernet at
0x1FF00000; guest RAM and native framebuffer use SDRAM/on-chip BRAM.
The upstream Main framebuffer contract starts at 0x22000000. The TV store
therefore retains its reservation [0x21800000,0x21C00000), with RGBx slot
bases 0x21800000, 0x2192C000 and 0x21A58000; maximum exclusive end is
0x21B84000. Avalon vbuf addresses are 16-byte words at `clk_100m`.

The burst arbiter gives ASCAL and TV separate masters, retains command
ownership through writes/responses and drains accepted bursts across reset.
One memory-domain owner prevents writes to DISPLAYING slots and holds the
selected image through both fields. Capture stages full 16-beat writes;
scanout prefetches 128/160-beat native rows.

LC lacked Quadra's startup vbuf gate. It is now included under the TV macro
ahead of the existing safe terminator. Normal-profile `sysmem` preprocesses
identically to LC HEAD. Normal `sys_top` routes also preprocess identically.
Unlike Quadra's TV branch, LC retains its existing HDMI pin path while TV
owns the analog pins. ASCAL internals and audio framework were not edited.

`CLAUDE.md` forbids local framework edits. The explicit request to port and
hook up Quadra's path was treated as authorization for the necessary narrow
hooks in `sys_top`, conditional core ports, TV startup gating and the DAC
constraint exception. No wholesale Quadra framework replacement was made.

## Constraints and checks

All LC CPU, peripheral and SDRAM timing constraints remain. The normal native
clock-group exception remains when the capture FIFO is absent. Buffered TV
uses the repaired Quartus-17 clock-target/fanout collection method, preserving
8 ns max-delay/skew budgets and hold cuts for both Gray buses. OSD payload
gets 30 ns, held-line mailbox 20 ns, and TV register-to-DAC propagation 10 ns.
Missing native Gray fanout, OSD or held-line endpoints fail closed. The broad
framework VGA output false path is omitted when the TV hierarchy exists.
No unsupported `all_registers -clock` was added.

## Compile still required

Simulation does not prove full mixed-language synthesis, RAM inference,
available PLLs, actual clock frequencies, TimeQuest endpoint/exception
coverage, memory-service worst case, guest boot/audio or CRT qualification.
The actual Ariel register protocol is not exercised by the V8 palette
fixture. No fit, LC resource headroom or all-domain timing result is claimed.

Clear authorization is required before Quartus because the earlier explicit
restriction against running it remains. Before an authorized build, check
all Quartus processes on this host, retain the global wait gate, and freeze
RTL/QSF/QIP/SDC/build stamp throughout the flow. Run A&S, then full fit and
STA; inspect capture FIFO, line RAM, OSD and write staging inference, PLLs,
all setup/hold domains, ignored/covered Gray and mailbox exceptions, DAC
timing, and ALM/M10K usage with ASCAL present. No hardware load is included.
